Beeper

A friend was fidgeting with some electronics at school and I helped him connect a battery, to a beeper, separated by a switch. From there I decided to take the fun idea and make it into a PCB design and a full product. I downloaded KiCAD and began without a clue for what I was doing. Within a couple minutes I called my electrical engineering father to guide me through finding components and checking datasheets. By the end of a few hours and with minimal understanding I had only found a few components but had found a wonder to explore the topic further. Over the next few weeks I was able to understand PCB design to an elementary proficiency.
