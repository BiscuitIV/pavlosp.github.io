This project began with the goal of creating a one meter wingspan RC plane that would be under 250 grams. The design was initially two carbon fiber tubes as the wing connected perpendicularly to another two carbon fiber tubes which served as the fuselage (the plane's main body). This system allowed for adaptible attachment of components which is important for maintaining balance (the center of gravity on a plane is intended to be at a specific length of the wing-shape/airfoil). That design relied on 3D printed parts as adaptors and for the elevator flap components. Each 3D printed component connected to the tubes via tension screws. All 3D printed parts were at first heavy and rigid, although with time I was able to improve at both designing and at setting up the 3D printer slicer. Overall I was able to reduce the weight of most 3D printed parts

Within a month I was able to make the first prototype with a bit of design help from a friend. In the process of making the first prototype I had rushed making a wing using paper and shipping tape. I had never flown a RC plane before and the "runway" that I was using was a rough asphault road. I crashed the plane three times before concluding that there was some sort of issue and so I continued improving the design: making components lighter, strengthening specific region, and so on. By this time I had printed many dozens of each component and had created a worlds off design from what I had began with. With the second round of test flights and a newly designed plane I had ordered more powerful batteries and a higher quality FPV drone motor. With this test flight I had gotten closer to success, being able to lift the plane off the ground for a few second. From here I went back to brain storming and before long I had done everything in my power to improve the plane and had taken a break from the project.

After going through the project weeks later I started to doubt the frame design for its non desired ability to twist and made the choice to switch to flat carbon fiber plates redesigning each component. This brings me to today where soon I will get upgraded electronics to finish this project off.
